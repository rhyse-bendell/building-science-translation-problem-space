# About the project

## Purpose

This project seeks to clarify the complex system through which scientific findings become useful beyond the research setting. Its focus is not a single transfer pathway, but the larger problem space that shapes translation into policy, programs, technologies, practices, and capabilities.

## What We Are Mapping

The work will identify relevant actors and settings, alongside barriers, facilitators, leverage points, and coordination needs. Together, these elements can show where translation succeeds, where it stalls, and where focused action may help.

## Expected Outputs

Planned outputs include structured concept maps, shared construct definitions, participant-informed interpretations, and practical materials that can support future research and action. Specific public outputs will be added as they are approved.

## Project Structure

An Advisory Group provides strategic guidance. Core Group members contribute sustained, structured input, while an Extended Group adds broader perspectives through asynchronous activities. The research team coordinates the study and synthesizes its results.
