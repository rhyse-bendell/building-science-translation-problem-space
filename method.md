# Method

The project uses **Group Concept Mapping (GCM)**, a structured, participatory approach for gathering ideas, organizing them, rating their significance, and interpreting the resulting maps. The process combines participant expertise with quantitative analysis while preserving a clear connection to the original statements.

{% include site_notice.html title="Process under development" text="Details and timing will be refined as study materials are finalized." %}

<div class="card-grid">
<article class="card"><h3>1. Planning and framing</h3><p>Clarify the scope, focus, participants, and intended uses of the project.</p></article>
<article class="card"><h3>2. Statement generation</h3><p>Gather concise ideas and examples in response to a shared focus prompt.</p></article>
<article class="card"><h3>3. Statement review and synthesis</h3><p>Consolidate, clarify, and prepare the statement set while retaining the range of perspectives.</p></article>
<article class="card"><h3>4. Sorting and rating</h3><p>Participants organize statements by similarity and rate them on defined dimensions.</p></article>
<article class="card"><h3>5. Analysis and mapping</h3><p>Analyze sorting and rating data to produce interpretable concept maps and patterns.</p></article>
<article class="card"><h3>6. Interpretation and outputs</h3><p>Work with participants to name, interpret, and translate the maps into useful outputs.</p></article>
</div>
