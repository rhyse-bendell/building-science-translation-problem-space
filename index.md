  <h1>Building a Problem Space for Science Translation</h1>
  <p class="subtitle">Team Science and the Translation of Research Findings for Application in the Real World</p>
</section>

This project examines how scientific findings move from research settings into real-world use. It will build a structured problem space for science translation by identifying actors, contexts, barriers, facilitators, and leverage points that shape whether research becomes usable policy, programs, technologies, practices, or capabilities.
{: .lead }

## Project purpose

The project brings together perspectives from defense, industry, and academia to develop a stakeholder-informed view of science translation. It uses Group Concept Mapping to elicit, organize, rate, and interpret stakeholder perspectives on this complex problem.

## What the project will produce

- A science translation problem map.
- A stakeholder-informed framework.
- Priority areas and leverage points.
- Briefings, reports, and scholarly products.

## Current project phase

The research team is preparing project materials and identifying candidates for Core Group and Extended Group participation.
