<section class="hero">
  <h1>Building a Problem Space for Science Translation</h1>
  <p class="subtitle">Team Science and the Translation of Research Findings for Application in the Real World</p>
</section>

This Army Research Office-funded project examines how scientific findings move from research settings into real-world use. We are building a structured problem space that identifies the actors, contexts, barriers, facilitators, leverage points, and coordination needs shaping whether research becomes usable policy, programs, technologies, practices, or capabilities.
{: .lead }
