<section class="hero">
  <p class="section-label">Project at a Glance</p>
  <h1>Building a Problem Space for Science Translation</h1>
  <p class="subtitle">Team Science and the Translation of Research Findings for Application in the Real World</p>
</section>

This Army Research Office-funded project examines how scientific findings move from research settings into real-world use. It will build a structured problem space by identifying what shapes whether research becomes usable policy, programs, technologies, practices, or capabilities.
{: .lead }

## What we are mapping

<div class="card-grid">
  <article class="card"><h3>Actors</h3><p>The people, organizations, and communities involved from discovery through sustained use.</p></article>
  <article class="card"><h3>Contexts</h3><p>The operational, policy, organizational, professional, and research settings in which translation occurs.</p></article>
  <article class="card"><h3>Barriers and facilitators</h3><p>The conditions that hinder translation and those that enable progress.</p></article>
  <article class="card"><h3>Leverage points</h3><p>Places where focused action may produce meaningful, feasible improvement.</p></article>
  <article class="card"><h3>Coordination needs</h3><p>The relationships, communication, alignment, and shared action needed across the research-to-use pathway.</p></article>
</div>
