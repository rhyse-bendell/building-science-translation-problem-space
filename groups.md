# Engagement groups

The project uses three complementary forms of engagement to include perspectives from across the research-to-use pathway. Each group has a distinct role and level of participation; not every contributor is expected to complete every project activity.

<div class="card-grid group-grid">
<article class="card">
  <h2>Advisory Group</h2>
  <p><strong>Role:</strong> High-level guidance on framing, stakeholder coverage, recruitment strategy, interpretation, and practical relevance.</p>
  <p><strong>Typical contribution:</strong> Review terminology and framing, identify missing perspectives, advise on priorities, and assess product usefulness.</p>
  <p><strong>Participation intensity:</strong> Periodic, focused consultation at selected milestones.</p>
  <p><span class="status-badge">Collaborator review</span></p>
  <a href="{{ '/groups/advisory/' | relative_url }}">View Advisory Group details</a>
</article>
<article class="card">
  <h2>Core Group</h2>
  <p><strong>Role:</strong> Deeper structured input throughout the Group Concept Mapping process.</p>
  <p><strong>Typical contribution:</strong> Examples, statement review, sorting, rating, interpretation support, and feedback on emerging products.</p>
  <p><strong>Participation intensity:</strong> Sustained participation across several structured activities.</p>
  <p><span class="status-badge">Activities not yet open</span></p>
  <a href="{{ '/groups/core/' | relative_url }}">View Core Group details</a>
</article>
<article class="card">
  <h2>Extended Group</h2>
  <p><strong>Role:</strong> Broader representation of stakeholder experiences, roles, organizations, and settings.</p>
  <p><strong>Typical contribution:</strong> Invited online brainstorming, sorting, rating, or feedback activities.</p>
  <p><strong>Participation intensity:</strong> Selected, primarily asynchronous activities.</p>
  <p><span class="status-badge">Activities not yet open</span></p>
  <a href="{{ '/groups/extended/' | relative_url }}">View Extended Group details</a>
</article>
</div>
