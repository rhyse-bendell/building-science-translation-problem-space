# Participant groups

Mapping science translation requires perspectives from different parts of the research-to-use pathway and different levels of engagement. The project therefore uses three complementary groups: focused advising, sustained structured participation, and broader asynchronous participation. Together, these forms of engagement help the research team develop credible findings without asking every participant to take on the same commitment.
