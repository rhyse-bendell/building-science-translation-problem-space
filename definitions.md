# Definitions

{% include site_notice.html title="Living glossary" text="These working definitions will develop through the project and should not yet be treated as final." %}

## Science translation
The processes through which scientific knowledge is interpreted, adapted, communicated, and used in real-world settings.

## Transition
The movement of a research result toward an applied setting, user, process, program, or capability.

## Defense-related research
Research with potential relevance to defense missions, organizations, personnel, operations, or capabilities.

## Operational use
Application in the settings, decisions, practices, systems, or missions where work is performed.

## Barriers
Conditions that impede, delay, or prevent effective translation.

## Facilitators
Conditions that support or accelerate effective translation.

## Leverage points
Places within a system where a focused intervention may produce meaningful change.

## Coordination needs
Requirements for alignment, communication, sequencing, or shared action among actors and organizations.
