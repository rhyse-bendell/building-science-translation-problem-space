# Advisory Group role

Advisors provide high-level guidance on project framing, stakeholder coverage, recruitment strategy, interpretation, and practical relevance. Their perspectives help the team keep the project appropriately scoped and connected to the settings where science translation occurs.

Advisory members are **not responsible for completing the detailed Group Concept Mapping activities** assigned to Core or Extended Group participants. The team may invite focused advice at specific project milestones.
