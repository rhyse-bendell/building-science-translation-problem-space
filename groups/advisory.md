# Advisory Group role

## Role of the Advisory Group

The Advisory Group provides high-level guidance as the project develops. Advisors help ensure that the framing is clear, the right stakeholder perspectives are represented, the language is appropriate across communities, and the planned outputs are credible and useful. Their role centers on direction, scope, recruitment, interpretation, and practical relevance.

## What advisors may be asked to do

- Provide feedback on project framing and terminology.
- Recommend stakeholder groups and recruitment priorities.
- Suggest potential Core Group contacts.
- Review selected prompts, meeting materials, and draft products.
- Offer high-level interpretation of major findings when needed.
- Assess whether emerging products will be understandable and useful in practice.

## What advisors are not expected to do

Advisors are not responsible for completing Core Group activities such as full sorting and rating tasks or detailed analytic interpretation. Those activities are assigned to other participant groups as appropriate. Advisors also do not manage the study or conduct its analysis.

## How advisory input will be used

The research team will use advisory input to refine the scope and language of the project, strengthen stakeholder representation, identify issues requiring attention, and assess the practical relevance of emerging products. Advice will inform research-team decisions rather than replace the study's participant data or analytic process.
