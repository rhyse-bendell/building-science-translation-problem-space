# Core Group

## Role of the Core Group

The Core Group is a smaller participant group that helps the research team develop a clear and usable description of the science translation process. Members bring perspectives from across the research-to-use pathway, including research, government, industry, program management, and applied or operational settings.

## Expected activities

Core Group members may be asked to:

- Share short translation examples from their experience.
- Contribute candidate statements about the science translation process.
- Review and correct the final statement list.
- Sort statements into meaningful groups.
- Rate statements on selected dimensions.
- Suggest cluster names and interpretation notes.
- Review draft frameworks, summaries, and recommendations.
- Clarify specific issues when needed.

The research team will provide instructions and timing for each activity.

## What the Core Group produces

Core Group participation produces examples and candidate statements, review comments, sorting and rating data, suggested names and interpretations for clusters, and feedback on emerging products. These contributions help connect the analysis to real experience across stakeholder settings.

## How Core Group input becomes project data

The research team will treat Core Group contributions as structured project data, review, and interpretation support. Input will help organize the problem space, identify priorities and gaps, explain patterns, and improve the clarity and usefulness of project outputs.

## What the Core Group does not do

The Core Group does not manage the project, conduct the analysis, or write the final report. The research team remains responsible for study decisions, data management and analysis, synthesis, and final products.
