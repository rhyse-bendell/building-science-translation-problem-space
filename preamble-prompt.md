## Current working preamble

This activity asks participants to draw on their experience with the movement of scientific findings into real-world use. The final preamble will define the intended scope, relevant contexts, and terms needed to respond consistently without narrowing the range of useful perspectives.

## Current working focus prompt

> **Placeholder:** Please consider the conditions, actions, relationships, and contexts that shape whether scientific findings become usable in the real world. The final focus prompt has not yet been approved.

## Response guidance

- Respond with one clear idea per statement.
- Draw on experience or observation; concrete examples are welcome.
- Use plain language and define specialized terms where needed.
- Do not include sensitive, classified, proprietary, or personally identifying information.
- Additional activity-specific instructions will accompany each invitation.
